About Blake's Tax Service

A personal approach to professional tax preparation.

Blake's Tax Service is a professional tax preparation practice serving individuals, families, self-employed workers, and businesses from our office on Bronxwood Avenue in the Bronx.

Led by Lloyd Black, the practice is built on a simple idea: every client deserves to be heard, treated with respect, and walked through their return clearly. We take the time to understand your situation, prepare your return with care, and make sure you understand what you're filing and why — so tax season feels manageable instead of overwhelming.

Many of our clients come back year after year. That's the kind of relationship we work to build: dependable, honest, and focused on your long-term financial well-being.

  • Professionalism & integrityHonest guidance and careful handling of your information.
  • Personalized serviceYou work directly with someone who knows your situation.
  • Attention to detailEvery return is reviewed carefully before it's filed.
  • Clear explanationsWe help you understand your tax obligations.
