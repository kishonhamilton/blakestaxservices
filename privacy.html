Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Blake's Tax Service (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use the contact or consultation form, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, the service you're interested in, and your message. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or other sensitive financial information through this website — please don't include them in the form.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule consultations.
  • To provide the tax preparation services you request.
  • To communicate with you about your appointment or return.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, which may collect technical information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy. If an online form delivery service is used to forward form submissions to us, that provider processes the information you submit solely to deliver it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so sensitive documents should be shared in person or through a method we arrange with you directly.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you from this website or to request that it be corrected or deleted.
